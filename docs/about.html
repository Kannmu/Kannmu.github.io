{{ profile.bio.zh }}{{ profile.bio.en }}

我目前在东南大学机械工程学院攻读设计学博士学位，研究重点包括超声触觉、眼动交互与脑机接口。工程背景让我倾向于通过可运行的软硬件原型来提出问题，并通过用户研究验证设计判断。I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Design at Southeast University, with current work spanning ultrasonic haptics, gaze interaction, and brain-computer interfaces. My engineering background shapes a prototype-led approach: build the system, frame the question, then test the design with people.
